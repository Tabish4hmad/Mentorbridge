How it works

A simple path from goal to guidance.

Students find mentors and send requests. Mentors accept, share notes, and keep the relationship active through messages and Inbox conversations.

Create account

Join as a student or mentor and complete the profile step before dashboard access.

Find mentor

Students search mentors by name and expertise to match their learning goal.

Send request

Mentors review pending requests and decide who they want to guide.

Stay connected

Connected pairs use notes, notifications, follow-ups, and Inbox chat.

Two dashboards

Built separately for students and mentors.

Each role gets its own focused workspace with the tools that matter most.

For Students

Find mentors, send requests, manage connections, receive notes, and chat from Inbox.

  • Search mentors by expertise
  • Track requests and connections
  • Read shared notes and messages

For Mentors

Accept student requests, share notes, send follow-ups, and support students in one place.

  • Review mentorship requests
  • Upload notes and documents
  • Message connected students
